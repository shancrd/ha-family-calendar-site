Family Calendar for Home Assistant

A private, self-hosted family dashboard.

This application connects a household's own Google Calendar to its own Home Assistant server on the home network. It shows the family's events on a wall tablet and phones, colour-coded per family member, and lets family members add events to the shared family calendar.

Who it is for

It is used only by the members of a single household. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app reads and creates events in the owner's Google Calendar. The data goes directly from Google to the household's own Home Assistant server and is never sent to any other service.
